Professional Overview

I am an Ottawa-based business administration and data analyst who keeps team-focused projects organized through clear communication and professional accountability. I hold a Bachelor of Commerce (BCom) and am committed to ethical and compliance standards. I emphasize coordinating schedules, stakeholder expectations, and cross-functional teams to make sure work is delivered accurately and on time.

My background combines hands-on coordination with practical business intelligence and foundational Lean Six Sigma principles. I use tools like Power BI, Power Query, and Tableau to build efficient tracking systems and dashboards that track project milestones, resource allocation, and compare operational KPIs.By integrating AI tools into my workflow, I improve repetitive administrative productivity, maintain strong data integrity, and transform raw operational records into actionable insights that help leadership make informed decisions.

To keep sharpening my organizational and analytical skills, I am currently completing the Business Intelligence & Data Analyst (BIDA) certification, building on my completed Canadian Securities Course (CSC). Together, these credentials give me a solid grasp of budget tracking, regulatory environments, and risk management—allowing me to immediately support Commissionaires Ottawa’s project delivery and operational teams.
